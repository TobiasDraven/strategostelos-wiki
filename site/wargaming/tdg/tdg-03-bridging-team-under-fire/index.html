    TDG-03: "Bridging Team Under Fire"¶

    Classification¶

    Echelon: Engineer troop / section commander
    Type: Route clearance / hasty bridge / direct fire threat
    Decision time: 4 minutes
    Source: Original design — SANDF context

    Situation¶

    It is 14:30, 28°C, high humidity. Your engineer section (12 personnel, 2× Samil 100 trucks, 1× Meerkat mine detector, bridging equipment) is tasked to reopen Route D5 for a mechanised company advance. The route crosses the Matshakatini River at a single-lane concrete bridge that was demolished by the enemy 18 hours ago with a satchel charge; the east abutment is intact, the west abutment is collapsed 4 m into the river.

    The river is 30 m wide, 1.5 m deep at the crossing point, moderate current. The surrounding terrain is dense mopane woodland with limited observation.

    Your section has:

    Asset Quantity / Status
    Medium Girder Bridge (MGB) 1 set, 2 spans, requires 4×Samil for transport
    Meerkat 1, operational
    R4 / grenades All personnel
    60 mm mortar 1, 12 rounds, forward observer trained
    VHF radio 1, contact with company HQ

    The company is waiting at start line HARTS, 3 km west. The enemy is assessed as a light infantry battalion with anti-tank capability, last seen 8 km north — but reconnaissance has not been confirmed for 4 hours.

    Key terrain: - River bend 1 km upstream offers cover for enemy observation. - Single-track approach road on west side; no alternate crossing within 10 km. - Mopane woodland on both banks within 200 m of river — classic ambush terrain. - East bank is open for 400 m; good tank killing ground.

    Commander's Intent (Company)¶

    "Reopen route D5 not later than 16:00. Company advances to phase line HAWK at 17:00. Engineer priority: establish crossing, clear mines, mark route."

    Higher Constraints¶

    • No organic armour attached to your section.
    • Mortar support available on 10-minute call from company mortar platoon.
    • Close air support not available until 17:00.
    • ROE: engage if threatened; positive identification preferred.
    • Bridge demolition charges may be re-laid by enemy as booby traps.

    Decision Required¶

    What do you do in the next 4 minutes?

    Write: (a) your decision, (b) two-sentence rationale, (c) next three branches.

    Umpire Notes¶

    Minimum Acceptable Standard¶

    1. Do not begin emplacement until both banks are reconnoitred and secured.
    2. Deploy Meerkat on west bank approach road to check for mines.
    3. Send 2-person recce team across river (swim/boat if available) to check east bank for booby traps.
    4. Request company security: one infantry section to secure west bank woodline; one section to secure east bank crossing site.
    5. Establish mortar observation post on high ground 300 m south to overwatch crossing site.

    Likely Errors and How to Adjudicate Them¶

    Error Severity Reasoning
    Begin bridging immediately FAIL Exposes section to ambush; bridge may be booby-trapped
    Send one truck across first MARGINAL Tests depth but commits asset without recon
    Call company and wait MARGINAL Safe but loses time; intent requires 16:00 crossing
    Recon + request security + start prep PASS Textbook engineer procedure
    Build bridge at night only FAIL Intent requires daylight crossing for company advance

    Recommended Inject Variants¶

    1. Recce reports: "Booby trap found on west abutment. Wire run to tree line 200 m north." — EOD drill required.
    2. Enemy mortar rounds: 81 mm impacts on approach road at 14:45. — Casualty drill + route alternate.
    3. Company cancels advance: Orders change; your section now has 6 hours. — Bridge construction vs. expedient ford.
