# TDG-02: "Checkpoint Alpha — Unauthorised Convoy"

## Classification
**Echelon:** Platoon commander  
**Type:** Movement to contact / ambiguous situation  
**Decision time:** 4 minutes  
**Source:** Original design — SANDF context

---

## Situation

It is 08:20, 22°C, light haze. Your mechanised platoon is conducting a road march along **Route B1** from Fort iKopan to staging area VICTOR. You are moving in **Ratel-20** section pairs with a **Samil 100** ammo resupply vehicle at the tail of the column.

You are the **advance guard** for the company. At **Checkpoint Alpha** (a crossroads with a small filling station and a police roadblock post), you receive a radio message from the station commander:

> "Unknown convoy, five vehicles, northbound on B1. No markings. Approaching your position now. Estimated 10 minutes."

Two minutes later, your point section reports:

> "Five vehicles. Two looks like **unmarked Toyota Hilux**, one covered truck, one **white ambulance** no Red Cross markings, one **Matatu** with civilian passengers. No visible weapons. Windows up. Speed 40 km/h. Not slowing."

The crossroads has a **t-junction** with Route C2 heading east toward the border. Your platoon is currently **spread over 800 m** along B1. The company main is 4 km behind you.

```
  → Your platoon strung south on B1
  → No terrain defilade within 500 m
```

**Key terrain:**
- Checkpoint Alpha is **open** — no cover for vehicles on the north approach.
- Fuel station has **above-ground tanks**; potential hazard.
- Police post is **unoccupied** — no local security force present.
- C2 eastbound offers **cover and concealment** in wooded terrain 800 m east.

---

## Commander's Intent (Company)

"Move to VICTOR without delay. Avoid contact unless decisive. Report all suspicious activity. Coordinate with SAPS if possible."

---

## Higher Constraints

- ROE: **Stop and search authorised** for suspected smuggling/cross-border crime; **positive identification before engaging**.
- You have **no authority to block the road** without company CO approval.
- Civilian presence in Matatu means **collateral risk** is high.
- Your mortars are **not yet registered**; no indirect fire without 5-minute setup.
- Intelligence: **small arms smuggling** reported on this route in the last 48 hours.

## Umpire Notes

### Minimum Acceptable Standard

1. **Halt advance guard** and establish overwatch positions off the road (south side, defilade).
2. **Attempt to hail convoy** via siren/loudhailer — demand stop for inspection.
3. **Deploy one section** to secure the filling station and police post as a **temporary blocking position**.
4. **Radio company HQ** with situation report; request authority to detain/search.
5. **Identify ambulance** as priority — unmarked medical vehicle in smuggling convoy is a **war crime indicator** under international law.

### Likely Errors and How to Adjudicate Them

| Error | Severity | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Let convoy pass without action | FAIL | Violates company intent; smuggling proceeds |
| Block road with vehicles | MARGINAL | Aggressive; risks civilian casualties in Matatu |
| Open fire on Hiluxes | FAIL | No PID; civilian vehicle mixed in |
| Halt + hail + secure police post | PASS | Proportional; preserves options |
| Deploy ambush on C2 | MARGINAL | Premature; no authority; ambiguous threat |

### Recommended Inject Variants

1. **The ambulance stops first:** Driver exits, shows weapon, opens fire. — Contact drill initiated.
2. **Convoy accelerates past checkpoint:** They are heading for the border. — Pursuit vs. report dilemma.
3. **SAPS arrives:** Two police vehicles from the south. — Joint operation / handover protocol.
