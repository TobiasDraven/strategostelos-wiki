# TDG-04: "Medevac in the Contact Zone"

## Classification
**Echelon:** Platoon sergeant / medical corporal  
**Type:** Casualty evacuation / triage under fire  
**Decision time:** 4 minutes  
**Source:** Original design — SANDF context

---

## Situation

It is 10:15, 18°C, moderate wind reducing visibility to 500 m in dust. Your **infantry platoon** is conducting a **search-and-clear** in the **Maluleke area** (bushveld, rocky kopjes, dense scrub). You are the **platoon sergeant** moving with 2IC and the **medic corporal** at the centre of the formation.

At **10:12**, **Section 1** (lead element, 4 men) triggers an **RPG ambush** at a rocky kopje 150 m north of your position. Return fire is immediate. The platoon commander has taken **splinter wounds to the right shoulder** and is unable to command.

Situation report from 2IC:

> "Section 1 has 2 casualties: Rifleman Dlamini — **GSA to chest**, not breathing. Rifleman van der Merwe — **GSA to left leg**, conscious, bleeding. One enemy RPG position suppressed. Enemy strength unknown. Small arms fire from kopje continues. Medic is with me. Platoon commander down."

Your platoon layout:

| Element | Position | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | North, at ambush site | 2 casualties, contact |
| Section 2 | South, not engaged | 8 men, intact |
| Section 3 | East, flank security | 8 men, not engaged |
| Platoon HQ | Centre, 150 m south of contact | You + 2IC + medic, intact |
| Company main | 800 m south, not yet committed | Company HQ + reserve |

You have **one ambulance** (Rooikat armoured ambulance) attached, currently at company HQ.

**Key terrain:**
- Rocky kopje provides **enemy defilade**; RPG team likely in a cave/crevice.
- **Bushveld** between kopje and platoon HQ limits vehicle movement.
- No **casualty collection point** established yet.
- Medic has **2× personal aid kits**, **1× morphine**, **no stretcher**.

---

## Commander's Intent (Company)

"Clear the ambush site. Recover casualties if feasible. Continue to objective DELTA. Report status to company HQ within 10 minutes."

---

## Higher Constraints

- **No medevac helicopter** available for 45 minutes.
- Armoured ambulance is **not armoured against RPG**; approach route is exposed.
- Company reserve is **not yet committed**; company CO has not approved casualty recovery operation.
- ROE: **protect wounded; recover if tactically feasible; do not break contact to do so**.
- Two casualties: **one expectant** (Dlamini, chest wound, not breathing), one **urgent** (van der Merwe, leg wound, bleeding).

---

## Decision Required

**What do you do in the next 4 minutes?**

Write: **(a) your decision, (b) two-sentence rationale, (c) next three branches.**

---

## Umpire Notes

### Minimum Acceptable Standard

1. **Assume command** — you are now the senior surviving leader; declare yourself platoon commander.
2. **Report to company HQ** immediately — 2IC or medic radios status and your decision.
3. **Establish a Casualty Collection Point (CCP)** 100 m south of contact, in cover.
4. **Send Section 2 to suppress RPG position** with flanking fire; **do not send Section 2 to recover Dlamini** without contact broken.
5. **Medic treats van der Merwe first** — tourniquet, morphine, prepare for CASEVAC.
6. **Request armoured ambulance** from company HQ with security element.
7. **Dlamini is expectant** — medic confirms; focus resources on salvageable casualty.

### Likely Errors and How to Adjudicate Them

| Error | Severity | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Send Section 2 to recover Dlamini immediately | FAIL | Breaks contact; more casualties; violates intent |
| Abandon Dlamini without confirmation | MARGINAL | Pragmatic but medic should verify expectant status |
| Withdraw entire platoon | MARGINAL | Abandons contact; loses initiative |
| Assume command + report + establish CCP + request CASEVAC | PASS | Standard platoon sergeant drill |
| Move entire platoon to kopje | FAIL | Exposes to RPG; no clear objective |

### Recommended Inject Variants

1. **Medic reports:** "Dlamini has faint pulse — he is salvageable." — Decision box restarts; both casualties are now urgent.
2. **Enemy withdraws:** RPG position abandoned; Section 1 secures kopje. — Full casualty recovery now feasible.
3. **Company orders:** "Platoon must continue to objective DELTA in 15 minutes." — Time pressure on CASEVAC.
