# TDG-01: "Dry Riverbed Ambush"

## Classification
**Echelon:** Section commander  
**Type:** Immediate action drill / ambush reaction  
**Decision time:** 3 minutes  
**Source:** Original design — SANDF context

---

## Situation

It is 06:45, 15°C, clear skies, good visibility. Your section is moving along a dry riverbed (wadi) on foot as part of a larger company movement to contact. The riverbed runs east–west; 3-metre banks on both sides with sparse thornveld. You have no communications with platoon HQ since entering the bed.

At the bend 200 m ahead, **two trucks and a light troop-carrying vehicle** are parked nose-to-tail, engine-off, on the north bank. Three figures are visible near the trucks; no weapons are obvious. Your section is currently **in column**, moving single file along the wadi floor.

Your section consists of:

| Group | Composition |
|---|---|
| Forward point (2 men) | 50 m ahead of you, entering the bend |
| Section HQ | You + 2IC, R4, VHF radio, 2× 60 mm mortar rounds |
| Rifle group 1 (4 men) | R4, 2× R1 grenades, 1× 90 mm rocket |
| Rifle group 2 (4 men) | R4, 2× 60 mm mortar rounds |
| Machine gun group (2 men) | Vektor SS-77, 800 rounds |

Platoon order of march placed you in **security reserve** for this leg.


**Key terrain:**
- Bend creates a **dead zone** 80 m long; no LOS from your position to the trucks until the forward point clears it.
- Wadi narrows to 15 m at the bend; widens to 40 m east of the trucks.
- North bank has partial vehicle cover at ±20 m from trucks.

---

## Commander's Intent (Platoon)

"Move through. Locate and fix any enemy. Avoid decisive engagement. Link up with platoon HQ at RV EMERALD by 08:00."

---

## Higher Constraints

- You have **no indirect fire** on call until RV EMERALD.
- ROE: **Positive identification required before engaging** — suspected insurgent cache, not confirmed hostile force.
- Casualty evacuation not available closer than 5 km.
- Your forward point has not reported.

---

## Decision Required

**What do you do in the next 3 minutes?**

Write: **(a) your decision, (b) two-sentence rationale, (c) next three branches.**

---

## Umpire Notes

### Minimum Acceptable Standard

1. **Halt the section immediately.** No further movement into the dead zone without reconnaissance.
2. **Deploy forward point to overwatch** the bend from the south bank (gain LOS without exposing column).
3. **Send one rifle group to high ground** on south bank for flank observation.
4. **Place machine gun group** in covering position at 100 m back from bend, oriented north.
5. **Attempt to establish radio contact** with platoon HQ to report and request guidance.

### Likely Errors and How to Adjudicate Them

| Error | Severity | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Move forward to "assess" | FAIL | Exposes column to dead zone; violates security reserve role |
| Open fire immediately | FAIL | ROE requires PID; civilians or peaceable persons may be present |
| Withdraw entirely | MARGINAL | Safe but loses initiative; platoon intent is movement |
| Send whole section to clear bend | MARGINAL | Overcommits; no reserve; no contact with platoon |
| Halt + deploy as above | PASS | Standard textbook reaction; matches intent |

### Recommended Inject Variants

1. **The forward point reports:** "Three armed males, AK-47, moving toward trucks." — Decision box restarts with PID achieved.
2. **The trucks explode:** IED detonation on north bank at 06:52. — Reaction drill becomes casualty evacuation + security.
3. **Platoon HQ calls:** "Enemy RPG team reported 1 km east. Accelerate movement." — Pressure test on tempo vs. security.
