# TDG-05: "Silent Net — HQ Under EW Attack"

## Classification
**Echelon:** Company second-in-command / signals officer  
**Type:** Communications / EW disruption / command post drill  
**Decision time:** 5 minutes  
**Source:** Original design — SANDF context

---

## Situation

It is 11:40, 24°C, clear. Your **mechanised company** is executing a **movement to contact** in the **Waterberg bushveld**. You are the **company 2IC** travelling with the **company HQ** element: **Ratel-25** command vehicle, **signals corporal** with **VHF/UHF radios**, **Samil 100** signals vehicle, and **two Ratel-20** security sections.

At **11:35**, all **VHF platoon nets went dead** simultaneously. The signals corporal reports:

> "No transmission on any VHF channel. Receivers show **static only** — no jamming tone, just noise. UHF to battalion still up. I think we are under **wideband noise attack** or a **repeater is down**."

Two minutes later, **battalion UHF net** reports:

> "Company Alpha — your last SITREP was received. All other companies report normal comms. Check your systems."

You have not received a SITREP from **Platoon 1** (advance guard) for **18 minutes**. You do not know if Platoon 1 is still moving, in contact, or cut off.

Your company is currently **spread over 2.5 km** along a single dirt road. The terrain is **rocky ridge line** on the north, **dense bush** on the south. No cell coverage. No alternate communication method pre-briefed.

```
   ↓
[Battalion HQ] ← UHF contact, 8 km
```

**Key terrain:**
- **Rocky ridge** on north offers enemy **observation and EW site**; dominant terrain.
- **Single dirt road** limits maneuver; company is in a **column formation** — vulnerable to ambush if Platoon 1 is engaged.
- **No dead ground** within 500 m for HQ displacement.
- **UHF to battalion is clean** — this is a **VHF-specific disruption**, not a total comms failure.

---

## Commander's Intent (Battalion)

"Company Alpha advances to phase line EAGLE. Contact and report. Maintain cohesion. Battalion reserve is 5 km behind."

---

## Higher Constraints

- **No pre-briefed alternate communication plan** — battalion expected VHF to be sufficient.
- **No cell phones** in the field; no satellite phones at company level.
- **Runner protocol** is authorized but slow through bushveld.
- **EW team location unknown** — could be on ridge, could be vehicle-mounted ahead.
- ROE: **engage if engaged**; **report all contacts**; **do not split company without commander approval**.

## Umpire Notes

### Minimum Acceptable Standard

1. **Establish UHF net with all platoons** immediately — UHF is confirmed working; use it as alternate until VHF restored.
2. **Send runner to Platoon 1** with written orders and **request situation report** via runner return.
3. **Deploy one security section** to **north ridge** to clear enemy EW site or establish observation.
4. **Task signals corporal** to **locate jammer** using direction-finding equipment; request **EW team support from battalion**.
5. **Issue fragmentary order** to all platoon commanders via UHF: **"Hold present position, establish security, report status."**
6. **Do not advance** until Platoon 1 status is confirmed and VHF is restored or alternate comms established.

### Likely Errors and How to Adjudicate Them

| Error | Severity | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Continue advance without Platoon 1 | FAIL | Violates intent; company may be piecemealed |
| Send HQ forward to find Platoon 1 | MARGINAL | Risky; HQ should not be forward in contact |
| Withdraw to last known safe position | MARGINAL | Safe but loses momentum; intent requires advance |
| Use UHF + runner + security section + DF | PASS | Restores C2; preserves company integrity |
| Assume Platoon 1 is destroyed | FAIL | Catastrophising; no evidence; violates command responsibility |

### Recommended Inject Variants

1. **Runner returns:** "Platoon 1 is in contact, holding. One Ratel damaged. Request support." — Decision box restarts with confirmed contact.
2. **EW team located:** Direction-finding pinpoints jammer 3 km north on ridge. — Direct action vs. bypass dilemma.
3. **Battalion cancels advance:** Orders change; company now has defensive mission. — Comms disruption becomes less critical; focus shifts to defence.
