# Scenario Design Process

1. **Understand the purpose** — What analytical question is the wargame answering?
2. **Define the setting** — Where and when does the scenario take place?
3. **Establish the actors** — Who are the key players and what are their goals?
4. **Create the narrative** — What sequence of events leads to the wargame's starting point?
5. **Set the conditions** — What are the initial force dispositions, terrain, and weather?
6. **Design the injects** — What events will disrupt and challenge player plans?
7. **Validate realism** — Does the scenario reflect plausible real-world conditions?

## Scenario Robustness

A scenario should be **robust enough** to offer players latitude to make decisions about issues important to the sponsor, without being overly restrictive or permissive.

- **Overly restrictive:** Players feel railroaded; exploration is limited
- **Overly permissive:** Players lose focus; the wargame drifts from its objectives
