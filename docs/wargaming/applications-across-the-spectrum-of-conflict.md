## Levels of War

The concept of "levels of war" guides the organisation and execution of military operations.

| Level | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Technical** | Understanding and improving technical aspects of equipment operation |
| **Low-Tactical** | Training individual soldiers or small units |
| **High-Tactical** | Training commanders to control larger units (battalions, regiments) |
| **Operational** | Planning and coordination of large-scale military operations |
| **Military Strategic** | High-level military planning and decision-making |
| **National Strategic** | Broader implications of military actions on national security and policy |
| **Regional/Global Strategic** | Impacts on regional stability or global peace |

## Beyond Conflict

Military simulation and wargaming also apply to:
- **Management** — organisational decision-making
- **Business** — competitive strategy
- **Competition** — Fittest Soldier-type events for morale and mental fitness
