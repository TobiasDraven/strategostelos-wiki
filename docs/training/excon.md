# Exercise Control Structures — EXCON

> *"EXCON serves as the implementation structure through which the Exercise Director will plan, control, and execute the exercise."*
> — Adapted from EXDESIGN Textbook, Chapter 4

## What Is EXCON?

**Exercise Control (EXCON)** is the organisational structure responsible for planning, controlling, directing, and monitoring an exercise. It is the mechanism through which the Exercise Director (EXDIR) ensures the exercise achieves its objectives in a safe, realistic, and environmentally responsible manner.

EXCON is sometimes referred to as the **"White Cell"** or **"Control Group"** — the body that remains outside the player bubble and governs the exercise environment.

---

## The Command Group

The Command Group is the senior leadership of EXCON, responsible for overall direction and decision-making:

| Appointment | Responsibility |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Exercise Director (EXDIR)** | Overall command of the exercise; responsible for achieving objectives within budget |
| **Deputy Exercise Director** | Assists EXDIR; represents EXDIR in their absence |
| **Exercise Chief of Staff (COS)** | Coordinates and aligns all EXCON cell activities; manages administration |
| **Budget Manager** | Financial advice and support to EXDIR from day one |
| **Personal Staff Officer (PSO)** | Assists EXDIR |
| **Exercise Warrant Officer** | Discipline, welfare, and regimental aspects for EXCON staff |

---

## Exercise Control Centre (ECC)

The **Exercise Control Centre (ECC)** is the physical and operational nodal point from which the EXDIR commands and controls all aspects of the exercise. It must be:

- Established before the arrival of participants
- Operational 24/7 for the duration of the exercise
- Restricted — out of bounds to all training audience members
- The last structure to demobilise after the exercise

**ECC responsibilities:**
- 24/7 oversight of the exercise
- Real-world incident response and consequence management
- Movement control (MOVECON) for forces to and from the exercise
- Message management (logging and dissemination)
- Location tracking and display of all participating units
- Daily SITREPs and update briefs to EXDIR
- Coordination with the Safety Cell

## EXCON Cells

EXCON is organised into functional cells, each responsible for a specific aspect of exercise control:

### Exercise Control Cell

Under the command of the **Chief Exercise Control (Chief EXCON)** or **Senior Controller**, this cell manages the flow of events and incidents:

| Appointment | Responsibility |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Chief Exercise Control** | Senior controller; third most senior post in EXCON after EXDIR and COS |
| **Event Managers** | Coordinate EXCON structures for specific events; appointed for larger exercises |
| **Controllers** | Introduce PTA to events and incidents; observe and report on performance |
| **Higher Control (HICON)** | Next higher HQ for the PTA; issues orders and directives |
| **Lower Control (LOCON)** | Virtual units under command of the FC; provides realistic feedback |
| **Flank Control (FLANKCON)** | Simulates flanking units and coalition forces |
| **Adjudicators** | Determine outcomes of battle engagements in two-sided exercises |

### Scenario Development Cell

Under the command of **Chief Scenario**, this cell develops and manages all narrative and scenario products:

- Narrative (General Idea)
- Road to War / Crisis
- Military Problem / Mission
- Scenario (Special Idea and initial situation)
- Vignettes / Situation Sketches
- Main Events List (MEL)
- Main Incident List (MIL)
- Control Matrix / Master Events Synchronisation List (MESL)
- Inject Control Sheets and Injects
- INTREPs, INTSUMs, and supplements
- Simulated media
- Rules of Engagement / Rules of Contact
- UN / AU Resolutions, MOUs, SOFAs
- Country profiles and character profiles

### Training and Evaluation Cell

Responsible for two distinct functions:

1. **Training:** Deliberate training plan to address identified gaps before the PTA is assessed
2. **Evaluation:** Assessment and evaluation plan to measure PTA performance against objectives

**Critical rule:** These are two separate plans and must not be combined.

### Response Cell

The Response Cell provides real-time opposing forces and external role players. It is colour-coded by actor type:

| Colour | Actor Type |
|--------|------------|
| White | AU, UN, international agencies |
| Grey | NGOs, local government, police |
| Blue | Own forces |
| Red | Enemy / OPFOR |
| Green | Alliance partners / host country |
| Brown | Local population |
| Black / Purple | Criminal groups |
| Orange | Armed groups (no clear alliance) |
| Yellow | Media (including social media) |

**Critical rule:** The Response Cell must not act independently or "rogue." All actions must be planned, controlled, and based on approved scenarios.

### Role Players (Enactors)

EXCON members appointed to enact specific characters or institutions:
- Rebel leaders, mayors, political leaders, NGO representatives
- Protesting communities, striking employees, roadblock operators
- Hostage-takers, suicide bombers, important EPWs

### Battlefield Preparation Team

Responsible for physical preparation of the battlefield:
- Target preparation (mock targets)
- Site preparation (trenches, command posts, bunkers, patrol lanes, crash sites)

### Battlefield Simulation Team (BatSim)

Responsible for battle effects simulation:
- Battle noise simulation
- Pyrotechnical effects
- Wound simulation (Moulage kits)

### Real-Life Support Cell

Responsible for all logistical support to EXCON, the PTA, and supporting elements:
- Accommodation and facilities
- Transport and movement
- Catering and sustenance
- Medical support
- Supply and replenishment

---

## Controllers

Controllers form the backbone of EXCON. They are responsible for:

- Managing and controlling inject and incident input at the tactical level
- Observing and reporting on PTA performance
- Coordinating planned activities with the Response Cell
- Ensuring safety regulations are adhered to
- Providing inputs for the After Action Report (AAR)

**Controller types:**

| Type | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Higher Control (HICON)** | Issues orders and directives; provides "enemy" and environmental inputs |
| **Lower Control (LOCON)** | Commands virtual units under FC; provides realistic feedback |
| **Flank Control (FLANKCON)** | Simulates flanking units and coalition forces |
| **Observer-Controllers (OBCON)** | Embedded with units; observe and report |
| **Observer-Controller Trainers (OCT)** | Embedded with units; observe, report, and train |

**Critical rule:** Controllers must not form part of the immediate chain of command of the unit to which they are attached.

---

## EXCON Size and Structure

The size and structure of EXCON depends on:

- Type of exercise (CPX requires fewer controllers than FTX)
- Size of the PTA
- Geographical spread of events
- Complexity of the scenario
- Level of realism required

**Critical principle:** A custom-built EXCON structure is required for each exercise. Generic structures or over-inflated control structures waste resources and create safety challenges.

---

## Identification

All EXCON staff and vehicles must be clearly identified:
- Distinct armbands, coloured jackets, or hats
- Marked vehicles
- Freedom of movement — no PTA, STA, or OPFOR may interfere with EXCON movement
