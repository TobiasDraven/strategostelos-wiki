# Detailed Planning Conferences

> *"Planning for an exercise should be conducted with the same sense of urgency and focus on detail as when planning for a real-time operation."*
> — Adapted from EXDESIGN Textbook, Chapter 13

## Overview

The **Planning Stage** — also referred to as **Detailed Planning** — is Stage 3 in the Exercise Design Process. During this stage, detailed planning takes place to ensure that the approved Exercise Concept developed during Stage 2 is developed into a comprehensive Exercise Plan that can be executed during Stage 4: Execution.

Exercise planning is a continuous process involving a wide range of complex activities that interact, often in indirect ways. For an exercise to achieve its objectives, these activities must be aligned and synchronised through a detailed Exercise Plan.

---

## Planning Conferences

The exercise planning process consists of several integrated planning conferences, staff liaison, site visits, and back briefs. These activities occur over a set period and aim to bring together key role players, stakeholders, and participants.

### Initial Planning Conference (IPC)

The IPC takes place after the Concept Development Conference and is initiated by the Exercise Director (EXDIR) with all of EXCON in attendance.

**Purpose:** Introduce the main planning group to the Exercise Concept and training package. Exchange information to allow the exercise management team and planners to fully understand each other's requirements.

**Key topics:**
- Introduction to the Exercise Concept based on exercise objectives
- Exercise Plan focusing on planned main events
- Training requirements (refresher, integrated, specialised)
- Proposed EXCON structures
- Exercise Support Package (Narrative, Scenario, MEL/MIL, Master Event Synchronisation List)
- Technical requirements (ICT, Legal)
- Public Information/Media and Visits
- Real-Life Support
- Evaluation and Assessment Plan

**Deliverables:**
- Confirmation of Exercise Plan layout and content
- Exercise Order/Directive
- Draft exercise plan
- OPFOR requirements defined
- EXCON Appointments

### Main Planning Conference (MPC)

The main planning event for the exercise. Plans drafted independently by the work groups are reviewed and integrated into the Exercise Plan.

**Objectives:**
- Finalise and issue the Exercise Order/Instruction
- Finalise the EXCON structure
- Finalise the Exercise Plan
- Confirm objectives
- Confirm international PTA/STA participants
- Finalise Real-Life Support requirements
- Confirm Security plan
- Finalise the Evaluation Plan
- Hand over the Military Problem to the Force Commander

### Final Planning Conference (FPC)

Convened to confirm that all planning goals have been achieved and to review and update the exercise plan should there have been any unforeseen incidents or changes.

**Deliverables:**
- Signed Exercise Plan (EXPLAN)
- Final objectives list
- Exercise Narrative/Scenario
- MEL-MIL (key events)
- Exercise manning document (EXCON Structure with appointments)
- Evaluation and Assessment plan
- Training and Mentor plan
- Real-Life Support plan
- Transportation plan, Medical plan, Communications plan
- Opening and closing ceremony, DV Day programme

## Work Groups

The task of detailed planning is delegated to specialised work groups. The exercise director appoints a work group leader for each group:

| Work Group | Responsibility |
|------------|----------------|
| **Exercise Control** | Control structures, procedures, and management |
| **Scenario** | Narrative, scenario, MEL/MIL development |
| **MEL/MIL** | Main Events List and Master Incident List construction |
| **Training, Evaluation and Assessment** | Training plan and evaluation/assessment plan |
| **Technical Support** | IT, communications, simulation systems |
| **Public Information/Media and Visits** | Media simulation, DV day, public affairs |
| **Real-Life Support** | Logistics, personnel, facilities, administration |

Work groups may be further divided into smaller sub-groups focusing on specific aspects or disciplines.

---

## Back Briefs

To guide and coordinate the planning process, the IPC includes daily and end-of-conference back briefs from the various work groups:

| Meeting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Morning Coordination Meeting** | Confirm activities and outputs required for the day; confirm corrections from previous day |
| **End of Day Back Brief** | Present completed products; update status on outstanding tasks |
| **Focused Feedback** | Exercise director requires focused feedback on key aspects with specific work groups |
