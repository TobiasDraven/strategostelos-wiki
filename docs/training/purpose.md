# Purpose of Military Exercises

> *"A military exercise is a training activity, manoeuvre, or simulated wartime operation conducted under simulated operational conditions involving planning, preparation, and execution."*
> — Adapted from EXDESIGN Textbook, Appendix

## Definition

A **military exercise** can be defined as:

> *"A training activity, manoeuvre, or simulated wartime operation conducted under simulated operational conditions involving planning, preparation, and execution. It is carried out for the purpose of training and evaluation of military personnel and capabilities."*

---

## The Five Purposes of Military Exercises

### 1. Training

Exercises provide training opportunities for forces, units, and military personnel to:

- Practise and hone competencies and skills
- Train elements in new doctrine, tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs)
- Train soldiers and commanders in problem-solving and military decision-making
- Enhance interoperability and foster good relations between different functional environments, Services, Divisions, and allied forces

### 2. Evaluation

Exercises evaluate and validate:

| Evaluation Target | Description |
|-------------------|-------------|
| **Fighting ability** | Skills and proficiency of soldiers and units |
| **Doctrine** | Relevance and effectiveness of doctrine |
| **Staff competencies** | Planning and decision-making capability |
| **Structures and procedures** | Organisational efficiency |
| **Equipment and weapon systems** | Performance and reliability |
| **Gaps** | Identification of existing and future training/doctrine gaps |

### 3. Force Preparation (Combat Readiness and Mission Readiness Training)

- Prepare and rehearse forces for operations or specific missions
- Ensure forces are ready for deployment

### 4. Development

- Serve as an experimental test bed
- Develop and test new TTPs, doctrine, and equipment
- Provide real-world feedback for capability development

### 5. Deterrence

- Serve as a show of force
- Demonstrate preparedness and capabilities
- Deter potential adversaries

### 6. Tool of Government

- Used by governments to gain influence
- Political signaling through military capability demonstration

## Checklist: Exercise Purpose Alignment

- [ ] Primary purpose identified and documented
- [ ] Training objectives linked to exercise purpose
- [ ] Evaluation criteria aligned with purpose
- [ ] Force preparation requirements addressed
- [ ] Development/test objectives stated (if applicable)
- [ ] Deterrence messaging considered (if applicable)
- [ ] Government tool implications assessed
