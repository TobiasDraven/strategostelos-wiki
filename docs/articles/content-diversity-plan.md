## Notes

- **First-hand accounts:** All articles must include verifiable first-hand sources (memoirs, official histories, after-action reports, interviews).
- **Sub-Saharan Africa focus:** African perspectives must be grounded in Sub-Saharan Africa. North African perspectives are supplementary only.
- **Asia/South America:** At least one article must be from South America (Brazilian cybercrime covers this).
- **Noetic domain:** The existing pages are based on Western theorists (Weick, Klein, Boyd, Endsley). The African and Asian articles must draw on non-Western military thinking.
